About

What is Unwrap?

unwrap is a tool designed to let you view all of your listening history on Spotify and let you organize it just how you want.

It is completly free and without ads for all users, with no pay walls or locked features.

We simply want you to have a fun time looking through your listening statistics.

How does it work?

There are two ways we collect data from Spotify:

  1. Spotify Authorisation - allows us to keep your listening history up to date and create a profile.
  2. Extended Data - allows you to extend your listening history in unwrap to include your lifetime statistic, by downloading it directly from spotify.
